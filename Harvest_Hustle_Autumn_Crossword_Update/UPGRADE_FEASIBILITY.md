# Upgrade feasibility — version 3

This comparison simulates 14 sessions, 10 planting actions/session, eight starting plots, free harvest/sale/upgrade actions, and the current crop prices. It samples 160 crop-focused upgrade/timing combinations, not all possible strategies.

| Crop-only strategy | No upgrades | Best sampled with upgrades |
|---|---:|---:|
| Carrots | 14,480 | 18,640 |
| Corn | 10,640 | 13,904 |
| Apples | 10,680 | 14,152 |
| Pumpkins | 9,600 | 12,880 |
| Golden Gourds | 5,000 | 6,464 |

The best sampled upgrade setup for each crop-focused strategy was **two market stalls purchased at session 0**. Extra plots were not competitive in these sampled strategies. This is a **balance warning**, not evidence the economy is ready for a prize event.

**Costs:** Extra Plot = 1,000 coins each (500 resale), maximum four. Market Stall = 1,700 each (848 resale), maximum two. Players start with 2,000 coins, so they can purchase either one immediately but not both simultaneously. A market stall's break-even future gross sales is approximately (1,700−848)/0.10 = **8,520 coins**, assuming the player would have sold the same produce without the upgrade. An extra plot must create at least **500 coins of additional net value** to cover its effective cost after resale, and needs spare planting actions and seeds to do so.

**Conclusion:** Upgrades are affordable, but **not well balanced** against each other, and carrot-focused strategies dominate under the new rules. Do not launch a prize competition until crop prices and upgrade economics have been rebalanced and tested.
