# Features added

1. Registration window before the seven-day farming period; registration closes at farming start.
2. Automatic session notices with one event-role ping every 12 hours.
3. Three daily objective achievement stamps, with no cash rewards.
4. Farm statistics and individual lifetime progress.
5. Host dashboard and individual transaction history.
6. Automated midweek top 10 and final top 10/champion.
7. Public top-15 leaderboard with personal rank below the top 15.
8. Existing high-reward economy retained unchanged.

**Note:** farming is asynchronous. Crops grow automatically; players must click to harvest/sell. Notifications are sent when the bot is online; Railway must remain running and have persistent storage.
